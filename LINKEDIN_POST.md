# LinkedIn Post — Munazara Launch

---

In 10th century Córdoba, scholars held formal debates called **munazaras** — structured, sourced, and judged. One of the most intellectually alive traditions in history.

I built an AI version. It's called **Munazara** — and it just went live. 🚀

Here's how it works:

You enter any topic — "AI will replace software engineers", "Nuclear energy is the best climate solution", "Remote work is better than office" — and two AI debaters go head to head through a full structured debate:

⚖️ **Opening statements** — grounded in live web sources
🔄 **Rebuttals** — each side attacks the other's claims
⚡ **Cross-examination** — pointed questions and answers
🏁 **Closing statements** — final arguments
🏛 **Judge verdict** — a neutral AI scores each argument and picks a winner

You can also **debate the AI yourself** — choose your side, make your case, and see if you can win.

---

**Why Munazara?**

Most AI assistants avoid taking positions. I wanted the opposite — AI that *commits* to an argument, backs it with citations, and can be cross-examined on it.

The name comes from the Arabic مناظرة — the classical Islamic scholarly tradition of formal disputation. It felt like the right name for a tool designed to surface truth through structured argument.

The result is surprisingly good for exploring complex topics. Running "the death penalty should be abolished" through it surfaced arguments I hadn't considered from either side, with sources I could actually verify.
